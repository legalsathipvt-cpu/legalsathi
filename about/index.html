Our story

Why we built LegalSaathi

Getting a simple affidavit or rent agreement usually means standing in queues, hunting for stamp paper and losing half a day. We built LegalSaathi so that people in Lucknow can place a request online, share what is needed and have the finished document delivered at their doorstep.

Our team of law graduates prepares every document, and our delivery is secure: password-protected digital copies or sealed hard copies handed only to you.

How it works

Four easy steps

1. Choose

Pick your document on the home page and see the checklist of what to keep ready.

2. Share details

3. We prepare

Our experts draft, check and arrange stamping or notarisation where applicable.

4. Delivered

Track the status online and receive your document at your doorstep or digitally.
